LeeSide
Welcome to the beta

You've been invited to try LeeSide before anyone else — a paragliding logbook that lives on your phone and works even with zero signal at the hill. Four quick steps and you're flying.

1
Open the app
Tap the link below on your phone — it opens straight in your browser, nothing to download from an app store.
chnevill.github.io/UpLift
2
Add it to your home screen
This makes it open full-screen like a real app, and is what lets it work offline. Takes ten seconds:
iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share icon
  2. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" (top right) to confirm
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap the menu (top right, three dots)
  2. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add" to confirm
3
Sign in with Google
Open LeeSide from your new home screen icon and tap Sign in with Google.
Your flight log is saved to your own Google Drive as a single file — LeeSide has no servers of its own, and nobody else, including Chris, can see your data.
One small thing to expect: since LeeSide is still in early testing, you may be asked to sign in again about once a week, with the same "unverified app" screen appearing again. That's normal, not a bug — just tap through it like the first time.
4
Join the telegram group
This is how we communicate — questions, updates, and a place for quick feedback or sharing sites with other users.
