⚠️ Flight Safety Disclaimer

LeeSide is a logbook, not a safety authority. Weather data, site notes, emergency contact information, and every other detail shown in the app come from third-party sources or from you and other pilots — none of it is verified or guaranteed accurate, current, or complete.

You are solely responsible for assessing conditions and making your own go/no-go decisions before every flight. Never treat the weather shown in LeeSide as a substitute for an official briefing, your own on-site observation, your training, or your judgment as a pilot.

Emergency information — nearest hospital, contact numbers — is provided as a convenience and may be outdated, incorrect, or unavailable when you need it. In a real emergency, always call your local emergency number directly.

LeeSide and its developer are not liable for any injury, loss, or damage arising from your use of the app or reliance on any information it displays. You use LeeSide, and you fly, entirely at your own risk.

The short version: LeeSide doesn't run its own servers to store your data. Your flights, notes, gear, and photos are saved on your device and in your own Google Drive — a place only you control. We never see it, sell it, or share it.

What we collect, and why

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture — used only to identify your account and connect to your own Google Drive. We don't collect this for any other purpose.

Where your data actually lives

Everything you enter into LeeSide — flights, notes, gear, photos, settings — is stored in two places, and only two:

  • On your device, so the app works fully offline
  • In a file in your own Google Drive, so it syncs across your devices and stays backed up under your account

LeeSide has no database or server of its own. There is nowhere else for your data to be.

Location data

LeeSide uses your device's location for features you choose to use — like sorting nearby sites by distance, pre-filling the closest site when logging a flight, or looking up the nearest hospital in Emergency. Your location is used in the moment to power that specific feature and is not stored or tracked over time.

Third-party services LeeSide talks to

Certain features send limited, specific information to outside services in order to work:

  • Open-Meteo — site coordinates, to show live wind and weather
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim & Overpass) — coordinates, to find the nearest hospital for Emergency lookups
  • Google (Sign-In & Drive) — as described above

Tapping a Windy, Google Earth, OpenAIP, or embedded YouTube link takes you to that service directly — LeeSide doesn't send them anything itself, it's just an outbound link.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising trackers
  • No analytics that track you personally
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone
  • No account or data is used for anything beyond making the app work for you

Your control over your data

Since your data lives in your own Google Drive and on your own device, you're always in control of it:

  • Sign out at any time from Settings
  • Revoke LeeSide's access entirely from your Google Account permissions page
  • Delete the LeeSide file directly from your Google Drive
  • Use "Reset Everything" in Settings to erase all local data on your device

Children's privacy

LeeSide is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continuing to use LeeSide after an update means you accept the current version.
